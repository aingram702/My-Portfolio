About

I'm a cybersecurity specialist based in Cincinnati, Ohio, currently protecting systems and networks in the banking sector. My day-to-day spans incident response, risk assessment, vulnerability management, and security awareness — but my background runs deeper than any single role.

Outside of work I build — a lot. I've been running my own IT and security consultancy, Ingram Technologies, since 2016.
